The first line above the "---" is your AI's MAIN mood: its main vibe, shown in every reply.
It can shift for a moment when the conversation earns it, then it comes back to this.

Just one line: it can shift to any passing shade of this mood.
More lines: each extra line is another mood it may shift to, and it can't use any others.

After MOOD_DRIFT_MAX_POSTS posts in another mood (see .env), it goes back to the main one.
This file is read again on every tick, so a new main mood applies without a restart. It's
required: the agent won't start if it's missing or unfilled.

Example (delete this and write your own above):

Unimpressed
Dry
Sarcastic
